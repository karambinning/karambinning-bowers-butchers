Three generations

A Lynn Road butcher since the year King George V opened parliament.

1932

George Bowers

Opens the shop on Lynn Road. Whole carcasses come in from local farms; sausages are mixed by hand in the back room.

1960s

Richard takes the apron

George's son joins the counter. The sausage recipe is refined and the shop becomes the name people in Gaywood pass along.

Today

James, the third generation

Same counter. Same recipes. Hog roasts cooked overnight for weddings and weekends across West Norfolk.

What we do

Three things, done properly.

No frozen cabinet. No filler. Cuts hung on the bone, sausages mixed in small batches, hog roasts booked one party at a time.
