What's on the block

From the counter,
the block, the bone.

Made on-site, sourced locally, cut to order. Here's what we do — pick up at the shop or call ahead.

Services · 01 — 06

Six things to come in for.

01

Award-winning sausages

Pork & leek, Lincolnshire, traditional thick, breakfast thin and seasonal specials — all mixed in small batches each morning and wrapped at the counter in butcher's paper. The recipe Richard finalised in the sixties, still made the same way today.

Made fresh daily

02

Hog roasts to order

Whole hogs slow-roasted overnight for weddings, christenings, summer parties, village shows and corporate events across West Norfolk. Booked in advance — call us with the date and the headcount and we'll talk you through it.

Phone to book

03

Traditional cuts on the bone

Norfolk beef hung on the bone for proper flavour. Free-range pork. Lamb in season. Sirloin, ribeye, brisket, topside, chops, shoulders, racks — broken down on the block at the back of the shop.

Counter or pre-order

04

Local game in season

When the weather turns and the shoots start, game comes in from local estates — pheasant, partridge, venison, rabbit. Prepared, plucked and hung properly so it's ready for the pot.

Seasonal · ask in shop

05

Special orders

A particular cut for a particular meal. A leg of lamb butterflied for the barbecue. A crown of pork for Sunday lunch. A box of mixed meats for a freezer fill. If you can describe it, we can probably do it — call ahead and we'll have it ready.

By phone · 01553 773 845

06

Christmas turkeys

Free-range Norfolk turkeys booked through November, ready for collection the week before Christmas. Geese and gammons too. We've been doing the Gaywood Christmas list for decades — get on it early.

Book Nov · collect Dec
